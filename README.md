# ក្រូចស្រុកយើង | Kroch Fresh
### Fresh Natural Citrus from the Farm  
**ក្រូចតែជ្រក់ & ក្រូចថ្លុង**

An elegant, fully Khmer-language online store selling fresh, chemical-free citrus fruits harvested directly from the orchard.

**Live Website:** [https://krochfresh.vercel.app/](https://krochfresh.vercel.app/)

---

## Overview

**ក្រូចស្រុកយើង** (Our Local Oranges) is a simple yet polished e-commerce landing page for a Cambodian fruit business. It specializes in two premium citrus varieties:

| Product              | Khmer Name     | Price          | Unit Options     |
|----------------------|----------------|----------------|------------------|
| Sweet Orange         | ក្រូចតែជ្រក់   | 4,000 ៛       | 1kg / 2kg / 5kg |
| Pomelo               | ក្រូចថ្លុង     | 5,000 ៛       | 1 / 2 / 5 fruits |

The site highlights natural farming practices, daily harvest, careful packaging, and reliable delivery.

---

## Key Features

- **Full Khmer interface** — all text, buttons, and forms are in Khmer
- **Product catalog** with clear photos, descriptions, and price tags
- **Interactive shopping cart** (add/remove items, quantity control)
- **Quick order form** with fields for:
  - Name
  - Phone number
  - Product selection
  - Quantity & unit
  - Delivery address
  - Notes
- **Direct ordering options**
  - Phone: **096 350 8320**
- **“Why choose us” section** highlighting freshness, farm origin, packaging, and delivery
- **About section** explaining natural farming (no chemicals) and daily harvest
- Responsive design with mobile hamburger menu
- Clean, nature-inspired UI (cream background, orange accents, farm photography)

## Purpose

This project serves as a modern digital storefront for a local Cambodian citrus farm, making it easy for customers to:

- Browse fresh products
- Place orders quickly
- Contact the seller directly via phone or Telegram

Ideal for small farm businesses that want a professional online presence without complex backend systems.
